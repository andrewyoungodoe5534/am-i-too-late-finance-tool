## Overview

Am I Too Late? Lifestyle Calculator offers a compact way to examine how income, expenses, and savings relate to one another. Instead of presenting only raw financial inputs, it turns them into lifestyle numbers that can help users consider everyday money decisions through a browser.

The project is aimed at users who need a straightforward place to work with important financial figures without installing desktop software. It operates in the browser, and the information entered into the calculator is not collected, saved, or sent elsewhere.
